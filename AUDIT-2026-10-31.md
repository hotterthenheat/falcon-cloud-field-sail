# Slayer — full repository audit (31 Oct 2026)

Ten read-only reviews of `main` (e99b3cc): five on the math, five on everything else (server and security, data
loading, every page's UI, repo/build/tests/docs). Nothing was changed. Every claim has a file and line under
`slayer/` unless it says otherwise; the biggest ones were re-checked by hand. Typecheck passes on both configs.

Labels: **BUG** = a concrete input gives a wrong result. **RISK** = plausible, needs one check to confirm.
Priority: **P0** blocks going public or loses money · **P1** wrong numbers a trader sees · **P2** quality · **P3** tidy-up.

---

## 0. Why it doesn't work on your PC right now

1. **The ThetaData terminal is not logged in.** The last log on your PC says "Invalid credentials". Terrain's price
   bars, Weigher, Paper, Backtest and all of Compass come through that terminal, so they show nothing until it logs in.
2. **The site doesn't tell you that.** The supervisor counts any non-200 answer as "up" (`theta/supervisor.mjs:105`),
   nothing reads the word "credential", so every page says "ThetaData offline — reconnecting" forever.
3. **History is short by design, not by your key.** Every chart window is a fixed count of *calendar* days ending
   today (`src/data/thetaTape.ts:33`): 1m = 6 days, and 5m–1h are folded from that same 1m tape, so 1h has about
   28 bars. Scrolling left never fetches older bars (`StrikeChart.tsx:684-691,1666-1676`). The key can give far more.
4. **Compass mostly couldn't fire on 29 Oct.** Its record shows 476 live marks and 1,830 "late" marks
   (`.cache/compass/2026-10-29.json`); late marks are never allowed to call. If the engine fails once at start, it
   stays down until restart (`server/compassApi.mjs:192-212`).
