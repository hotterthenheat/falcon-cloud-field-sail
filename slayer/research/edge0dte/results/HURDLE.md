# EDGE0DTE hurdle (PROTOCOL section 5) - DISCOVERY only

Input: trades.parquet (foundation.trades): executable round trips (1-second NBBO, ask in / bid out at the first valid
quote >= t + 2 s, $0.65/contract/side) of the 0.30-delta call, the 0.30-delta put and the ATM straddle, held to h.

Per root x 30-min bucket x h (and pooled over buckets):
  cost       round-trip cost / premium = (entry half-spread + exit half-spread + fees) / premium (median, mean)
  direction  OLS over single options (calls and puts pooled), day-clustered:
                 ret_net = a + b * za + c * (V - 1)          za = sign-aligned Z (call +Z, put -Z)
             hurdle E[za]* = -(a + c * (mean V - 1)) / b : the sign-aligned mean Z a long 0.30-delta option needs for
             zero expected net P&L when realized variance stays at its average for that cell
  volatility OLS over straddles:  ret_net = a + c * (V - 1);  long hurdle (E[V] - 1)* = -a / c
             short-premium (iron-fly proxy): OLS ret_mid = a_m + c_m (V - 1); a short straddle pays the same
             round-trip cost, so it breaks even at (E[V] - 1)* = -(a_m + mean cost) / c_m (V must be BELOW 1 + this)
  CIs: day-clustered bootstrap (500 resamples of days) of each hurdle.

Sample (fixed before any fill was seen, foundation/q1s_plan.py): SPXW every DISCOVERY expiry session at 10:00, 12:00 and 14:00 plus 200 sessions per other 30-min bucket at a random grid time (100 from plan_base, seed 7, + 100 from plan_hurdle2, seed 17); SPY/QQQ/IWM 30 sessions per bucket (plan_base, seeds 11-13) plus, where fetched, every DISCOVERY session at 10:00/12:00/14:00 (the baseline rows). "SPY+QQQ+IWM" pools the three ETF roots (inference still clustered by trading day). Bucket "09:30" holds the 09:45-09:55 decision times. Exit decision min(t + h, close - 2 min); "close" = 15:58:00 decision (the Stage-1 Z/V at h=close run to 16:00). Labels: fills OBS; returns, Z, V, hurdles CALC.

Reading: hz = sign-aligned E[Z] a Stage-1 directional effect must reach before a long 0.30-delta option can make money after costs; hv_long = E[V]-1 a long straddle needs; hv_short = E[V]-1 below which a short straddle (iron-fly proxy, before the wing cost) makes money. A Stage-1 effect smaller than its hurdle cannot be an option edge. "-" = fewer than 30 round trips in the cell, or the fitted slope (b or c) is not positive so no finite hurdle exists (the structure does not gain from the outcome in that cell). Costs are the median and mean of (entry half-spread + exit half-spread + fees) / premium; the mean at "close" is inflated by winners: an option that ends deep in the money is exited across a wide ITM spread (SPXW DISCOVERY: 10% of single-option round trips to the close cost > 27% of premium; those above 30% have a median mid-to-mid gain of +315%). The 15:30 bucket holds only 15:30, so its 30-min and close exits coincide; h = 60 is undefined after 15:00.
