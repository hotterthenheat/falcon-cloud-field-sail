## Criteria (all required)

| # | criterion | exact rule used |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | registered sign | effect has the REGISTRY sign (`expected_sign`). Wrong-sign cells never pass. D5 was registered two-sided (ln V != 0 per bucket), so its direction is the sign of its effect and the hurdle side follows it. |
| 1 | FDR | BH q <= 0.10 over the m cells. |
| 2 | years | among 2020-21 (pooled), 2022, 2023, the periods with >= 30 sessions in the cell; >= 2 of them have the per-period effect in the registered direction (fewer than 2 such periods = fail). |
| 3 | hurdle | point estimate vs `results/HURDLE.md` at that horizon. Z rows: the raw sign-aligned conditional mean E[za] of the events (what a long 0.30-delta option is paid on; X rows: the predicted-stronger group; BF X rows only supply the bucket-demeaned group mean; C X3/X11: the za-equivalent of the OLS coefficient) >= |E[za]|*. V rows: conditional E[V]-1 > the long hurdle (registered ln V > 0) or < "short profits only below" (registered ln V < 0). Hurdle used: the composition-matched one where the family supplied it (AE: median of the bucket hurdles over the events; BF: event-weighted bucket hurdle; D1-D4: `*_mix`, bucket hurdles weighted by the condition's time-of-day mix); D5/D6: their own 30-min bucket; C: pooled (bucket "all", none supplied). Thin-cell rule (D family): a bucket hurdle resting on < 100 times falls back to the pooled one (no SPXW bucket is thin: every SPXW bucket hurdle rests on >= 198 times). The pooled-hurdle verdict is also shown (`H pooled`); it changes no survivor verdict unless noted. |
| 4 | replication | the identical cell (row, arm, outcome, h, P) on SPY, QQQ, IWM: >= 2 of 3 point estimates in the registered direction. A replication cell that does not exist (C1/C2/X3/X11 need trade flow: SPY only) counts as not replicated. |
