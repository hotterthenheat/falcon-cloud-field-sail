# EDGE0DTE Stage 1, family AE: dealer gamma regimes (A1-A7), dealer hedging (E1-E4), GEX-conditional tests (X4, X7-X10)

DISCOVERY only (expiry sessions 2020-02-18 .. 2023-12-31). No CALIBRATION (2024), HOLDOUT (2025) or 2026 outcome was computed or read: the FOUNDATION panel is filtered to split == DISCOVERY on load and the feature build refuses later sessions. Raw statistics only: no FDR, no pass/fail (the aggregator applies BH-FDR across all families). `results/stage1/AE.parquet` (+ `.csv`) holds every cell; this file is its readable form.

**Every A, E and X result here is MODEL**: dealer positioning is not observed. REGISTRY convention: customers are net long puts and net short calls, so dealers are long call gamma and short put gamma; GEX(K) = gamma(K) x (OI_call - OI_put) x 100 x S^2 x 0.01, gamma from Black-76 on the observed board mid at t (lib.dealer). P = same-day-expiry OI; G:oi7 = all expiries <= 7 DTE (later expiries get the same strike's same-day IV: flat term structure, MODEL-G). OI = OI published for the day before its open (OBS). Inputs: board, bars, OI = OBS; IV, Z, V, RV, B7/D1/D4 legs = CALC; GEX, flip, walls, concentration, charm/vanna/gamma hedge flows = MODEL.

## Method

* Decision grid: every 5 min 09:45-15:30 ET (FOUNDATION panel); outcomes Z, V at h = 15, 30, 60 min and the close (PROTOCOL section 4); A3 G:1-min uses 1-minute event times with Z from lib.outcomes.zv.
* Effect, Z rows: mean over events of s x (Z - mu_b), s = the feature direction, mu_b = unconditional mean of Z in the same 30-minute bucket over all DISCOVERY grid rows of the root. V rows: mean of (ln V - mu_b). A1 and X rows are differences between the GEX regimes, oriented so that the registry prediction is a positive effect. GEX regime = sign of total GEX at 09:45 of the session (as in BASELINES regimes); "G:GEX at t" uses the sign at the decision time.
* CI and p: day-clustered bootstrap, 2000 resamples of trading days, the bucket means re-estimated in every resample. p two-sided. E3 is registered as incremental: its p_value is from OLS of (Z - mu_b) on sign(hedge flow) and the prior-15-minute Z with CR1 day-clustered errors (the bootstrap p is kept in `boot_p`).
* IC: Spearman correlation of the continuous feature with the demeaned outcome (A2: |dist|, A5: PIT percentile, A6: PIT-scaled dGEX, E1-E4: PIT-scaled flows); CI = day-clustered bootstrap (2000) of the Pearson correlation of the full-sample ranks.
* Point-in-time (PIT) scaling: A5 terciles, A6 "sd", D4 terciles (X7) and the flow scales for the IC use the same root's values in the same 30-minute bucket over the PRIOR 20 expiry sessions (>= 10 sessions, >= 30 values; the day itself excluded; scale = RMS about zero). The first ~10 sessions of each root have no PIT value.
* Per-year effects: same formula on 2020-21 pooled, 2022, 2023 (sessions in parentheses); bucket means from the full DISCOVERY sample.
* Hurdle (results/HURDLE.md, hurdle.parquet): Z rows |E[za]|* pooled over buckets for that root/h, and in parentheses the median of the bucket hurdles over the events; V rows E[V]-1 long hurdle / short-below hurdle (pooled). `cond raw` = raw mean sign-aligned Z (Z rows) or raw mean V - 1 (V rows) of the events (for difference tests: the group predicted stronger). `beyond hurdle` (descriptive only): Z rows |effect| > |E[za]|*; V rows cond raw > long hurdle (effect > 0) or < short-below hurdle (effect < 0). `CI excl 0` and `beyond hurdle` are NOT a pass: FDR, the year rule and replication are the aggregator's.
* In the FDR family: SPXW x param P (column `in_fdr_family`). SPY/QQQ/IWM = replication; G and diag rows = sensitivity, never used to choose.

## Mechanisms and exact definitions

| row | feature at t | mechanism | outcome, predicted sign |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | total GEX sign at 09:45 (same-day OI) | long-gamma dealers damp, short-gamma dealers amplify | V: ln V(GEX<0) - ln V(GEX>0) > 0 at every grid time of the session; Z: prior-30-min direction continues when GEX<0 and reverts when GEX>0 (one test, s = sign(z_prev30) x -sign(GEX)) |
| A2 | |S - flip| / (S sigma_close) < 0.5 | hedge convexity largest near the flip | V: ln V above the same-bucket unconditional mean; a session time with no flip inside +/-3 sigma_close counts as far (|dist| = 3) |
| A3 | 5-min close on the other side of the flip than the previous 5-min close (flip recomputed at each) | regime switch at the flip | Z in the crossing direction > 0 |
| A4 | within 0.5 sigma_30 of the call wall (above S) or put wall (below S); nearer wall if both | hedging concentrates at the wall: approach stalls | Z toward the wall < 0 |
| A5 | Herfindahl of |GEX(K)| within +/-2 sigma_close, top tercile vs the prior 20 sessions (same bucket) | concentrated long gamma pins | ln V < 0 |
| A6 | GEX(S_t) - GEX(S_09:45) with the table at t, as a fraction of gross |GEX|, scaled by its PIT RMS; < -1 | walking into short gamma raises RV the board has not repriced | ln V > 0 |
| A7 | t >= 14:00: direction to the largest-|GEX| strike within 1 sigma_close | expiry-day pinning | Z_close toward it > 0 |
| E1 | sign of charm hedge flow t->close (shares dealers must buy), t >= 13:00 | decaying OTM delta unwinds hedges | Z_close in its sign > 0 |
| E2 | sign of the vanna hedge flow of the last 30-min IV_atm change | IV change moves dealer delta | Z_30, Z_60 in its sign > 0 |
| E3 | sign of -Gamma_dealer x dS(15 min) | dealers trade with (short gamma) or against (long gamma) the move | Z in its sign > 0, incremental over the prior-15-min Z (OLS, day-clustered: p from the OLS) |
| E4 | sign of H = E1 + E2 + E3 (shares) | the sum is what dealers must trade | Z in sign(H) > 0; IC > 0 |
| X4 | B7 first-30-min return incl. gap -> last-30-min Z, by GEX(09:45) sign | short-gamma hedging amplifies late-day momentum | effect(GEX<0) - effect(GEX>0) > 0 |

| X8 | A7 pin, by GEX(09:45) sign | pinning needs long gamma | effect(GEX>0) - effect(GEX<0) > 0 |
| X9 | D1 RV30 / prior-20-session same-time median > 2, by GEX(09:45) sign | RV expansion persists under short gamma | ln V(GEX<0) - ln V(GEX>0) > 0 |

Implementation notes (choices the registry leaves open, fixed before any outcome was computed):
* A1/X: the regime is the 09:45 GEX of the session and applies to every grid time of that session; "|09:45 only" diag rows use the 09:45 decision alone.
* A2: flip = lib.dealer flip (re-priced on +/-3 sigma_close, crossing nearest S); no crossing -> far.
* A3 G:1-min: the flip is recomputed every 5 minutes (the latest grid time <= the minute), not every minute (cost); a crossing can come from the flip moving as well as from spot (both P and G).
* A4: distances (wall - S)/(S sigma_30) and (S - put wall)/(S sigma_30); call wall = strike above S with the largest positive GEX, put wall = strike below S with the largest |GEX| (lib.dealer).
* A6: "re-priced at t vs open" = the strike table at t (IVs and T at t) re-priced at S(09:45); the change divided by gross |GEX| at t, then by its PIT RMS.
* A7/X8: every grid time >= the start (14:00 P; 13:00, 15:00 G); strikes with |ln(K/S)| <= sigma_close; ties (S = K) skipped.
* E1/X10: every grid time >= 13:00 (12:00, 14:00 G); 13:00-close days have no such time.
* E3 G lags 5/30 use the same prior-15-minute control as P (as registered).
* E4: H = charm_flow + vanna_flow(30) + gamma_flow(15), all in shares, defined from 10:00 (vanna lag); G drops E1.
* X4: B7 = ln(S(10:00) / prior session close) (P; G: / open of the 09:30 bar); decision = close - 30 min, Z over the last 30 minutes (h = 30 = to the close).
* X7: D4 = IV_atm(t)^2 / (RV of the last 60 one-minute returns, annualised), from 10:30; lowest PIT tercile (G: quintile). X9: D1 = RV of the last 30 one-minute returns / median of the same at the same minute over the prior 20 NYSE sessions (>= 10 needed); > 2 (G 1.5, 3).
* Speed only: families/ae_dealer.py caches the OI pivot of lib.dealer.strike_table per session; the result is asserted identical to lib.dealer.strike_table value by value in the leakage test.

## N/A and limits

* A7, E1, X8, X10: registered at h = close only; X4 at the last 30 minutes only; E2 at h = 30 and 60 only. Other horizons are not tests of these rows (not N/A, not computed).
* E1/X10 on 13:00 early-close sessions: no decision time >= 13:00 (P) - those sessions do not enter.
* True dealer inventory, intraday OI and customer identity: N/A (PROTOCOL section 2); the dealer side is the MODEL convention, so every A/E/X result inherits that assumption.
* G:oi7: boards of later expiries are not cached, so their IV is the same-day IV of the strike (flat term structure, MODEL-G).
* PIT features (A5, A6, X7 and the E ICs) are NaN for the first ~10 expiry sessions of each root.
* Per-year cells with fewer than 30 sessions are reported, not hidden (IWM and QQQ 2020-21 have few 0DTE sessions).
