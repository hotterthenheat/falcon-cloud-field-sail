## Statistic

* Sign-aligned rows: value = d * (y - mu_t), y = Z_h or ln V_h, d = the registry direction (+1/-1), mu_t = mean of y at the same decision time over the rows where the feature is observable (the unconditional same-time mean). effect = mean value over signalled rows; 95% CI and two-sided p from a day-clustered bootstrap (2,000 resamples of days, lib.stats.boot_mean). `exp` = the registry sign of effect (C4/C5 Z rows predict a NEGATIVE effect).
* C7 (unsigned V row): value = ln V - mu_t over event rows.
* IC: Spearman rank correlation of the continuous feature with y - mu_t over the observable rows, day-clustered bootstrap CI (2,000). C1 IC uses ln(rc/rp); C6 IC uses the normalised OI change.
* X3, X11: OLS of Z_h on the feature + controls with CR1 day-clustered errors (lib.stats.ols_cluster); effect = coefficient, CI = coef +/- t(G-1) se, p from the clustered t. `za-eq` = coefficient x mean |feature| (X3) or the coefficient per event (X11), the size compared with the hurdle.
* Hurdle: results/HURDLE.md, root x h, bucket "all" (hz = |E[za]| for Z; E[V]-1 long / short-below for V; for V rows `cond` = mean V - 1 of the predicted-high group, compared with the hurdle). `beyond` = CI excludes 0 AND the size clears the hurdle (information only); cells with < 30 signalled observations (`n ev`, marked `thin`) are never flagged. `sign` says whether the effect has the registry sign. For V rows `uncond` = mean V - 1 over all observable rows: when it already sits beyond the short hurdle (the unconditional variance premium), a V flag mostly restates that premium rather than the feature.
* Per-year columns: mean value (OLS rows: coefficient, if >= 30 sessions) and n obs, 2020-21 pooled, 2022, 2023.

## Data and labels

* Z, V, controls: the FOUNDATION panel (CALC). Board (1-minute NBBO snapshot with sizes): OBS; IV, delta, skew, spreads: CALC. OI: OBS (published before the open; lib.data.oi drops rows stamped >= 09:30). Flow: OBS prints, aggressor side CALC (quote rule).
* C1 uses ALL kept prints (`volume`: single-leg, multi-leg, late, bad-quote). C2/X3 use the classified single-leg subset only: `vol_buy - vol_sell` (strict quote rule; at-mid prints, the Lee-Ready tick test, multi-leg (35-45% of SPXW, ~8% of SPY volume), late and bad-quote prints are excluded).

No look-ahead (leakage-tested).

## Mechanisms

* **C1**: unusual 0DTE call vs put volume (last 15 min / 20-session same-minute median): informed demand shows up in volume before price; Z toward the heavier side > 0
* **C2**: aggressive delta flow (quote-rule signed single-leg volume x delta x 100, last 15 min, standardised): buy-initiated delta forces dealers to hedge the same way / carries information; Z in flow direction > 0
* **C3**: near-ATM (|delta| 0.2-0.5) board size imbalance, calls minus puts: resting liquidity imbalance precedes quote moves; Z toward the imbalance > 0 at h = 15
* **C4**: residual ATM IV change (15-min change minus the prior-20-session spot-vol fit): option-led information; Z < 0 and ln V > 0 when the residual is up
* **C5**: 15-min change of 25-delta put minus call IV: crash-protection demand leads the index; Z < 0 when skew rises
* **C6**: OI_D - OI_D-1 of puts minus calls within +/-2% of the open (pre-open data): new positioning carried overnight; put build -> Z > 0 to the close (hedged dealers)
* **C7**: near-ATM spread/mid vs its 20-session same-minute median: wide spreads = market makers see risk; ln V > 0 when the ratio > 1.5
* **X3**: C2 flow incremental over prior return (z_prev30), ETF volume (ln ratio), prior RV (ln ratio) and time-of-day bucket FE: coefficient > 0
* **X11**: C1 unusual volume (signed event) incremental over B6 momentum (z5 and the B6 signal), ETF volume, prior return, prior RV and bucket FE: coefficient > 0
