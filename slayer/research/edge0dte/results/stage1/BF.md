## Definitions

* Bars: 1-minute OBS bars (SPX index for SPXW, the ETF otherwise); 5-minute bars aligned at 09:30, usable when their last minute has closed. Swing (REGISTRY F): a 5-min high strictly above the 2 highs on each side (low mirrored), within the session; CONFIRMED at the close of bar i+2 and never used before (tested). Structural decisions at bar k use swings confirmed by the close of bar k-1.
* Decision time t = the close of the event bar (5-min boundary; F7/F8 retests = the close of the 1-minute bar that traded into the zone). Only events with 09:45 <= t <= close - 30 min (the Stage-1 grid window) are tested; events of one (row, param) at the same (day, t) are merged (opposite directions dropped). State rows (B4, B5, B6, B8, F1) are evaluated at every grid time where the state holds.
* Outcome Z(t,h) = ln(S(t+h)/S(t)) / (IV_atm(t) sqrt(h)) (CALC, lib.outcomes). effect = mean over events of dir x (Z - mu), mu = the unconditional DISCOVERY mean of Z_h of the same root over all grid times of the same 30-minute bucket; `mean_za_raw` (parquet) = the same without mu. CI/p: day-clustered bootstrap, 2,000 resamples (lib.stats.boot_mean); X rows: difference between regimes (lib.stats.boot_diff). Per-year columns: 2020-21 pooled, 2022, 2023 with n events and sessions.
* Hurdle: HURDLE.md hz (the |E[za]| a long 0.30-delta option needs) for the root, the event's 30-min bucket and h, averaged over the events (missing cell -> pooled cell); X rows use the predicted-stronger regime's events. expected_sign = +1 for every cell (dir is the registry's predicted direction; X: predicted-stronger regime minus the other).
* Labels: bars, ETF volume, VIX = OBS; VWAP, EMAs, swings, ATR, Z = CALC; GEX sign and call/put walls = MODEL (lib.dealer.exposures, same-day OI, REGISTRY dealer convention: dealers long calls / short puts). **SPXW B3a/B3b use SPY's session VWAP and SPY's 5-minute closes (SPX has no volume): labelled "SPY VWAP proxy"**; the band uses the SPXW board's sigma_30 (a log distance, so unit-free). VIX tercile: VIX 1-min close at t vs the 1/3, 2/3 quantiles of the prior 252 sessions' daily VIX closes (point-in-time, >= 40 prior closes).

* **B1** opening-range breakout: first 5-min close beyond the 30-min OR (P OR30; G OR15, OR60); one event per session
* **B2** failed breakout: every fresh 5-min close beyond the OR30 (previous close inside), then a 5-min close back inside within W min (P 30; G 15, 60); dir = toward the opposite OR side
* **B3a** VWAP acceptance: N consecutive 5-min closes beyond session VWAP, the first of them the crossing close (P 3; G 2, 4)
* **B3b** VWAP rejection: previous 5-min close on one side, bar low/high within tol x sigma_30 of VWAP, close beyond the band (P 0.1; G 0.05, 0.2); dir = away from VWAP
* **B4** EMA alignment: last complete bar close > EMA9 > EMA21 on all of 1/5/15/30/60-min (or all inverted), EMAs warmed on 10 prior sessions; state at every grid time (P 5 of 5; G >= 4, >= 3 of 5 with none opposite)
* **B5** trend exhaustion: |ln(S/open 09:30)| > k x IV_atm(t) sqrt(minutes since open) and last-15-min return opposite; state on the grid (P 1.5; G 1.0, 2.0); dir = against the day
* **B6** momentum acceleration: |Z_5min| > k, 5-min and 15-min returns same sign, |r5|/5 > |r15|/15 (per minute); state on the grid (P 2; G 1.5, 2.5)
* **B7** intraday momentum: sign of ln(S(10:00)/prior close) (P, with gap) or ln(S(10:00)/09:30 open) (G); event at close - 30 min, outcome Z to the close; IC = Spearman(return, Z_close)
* **B8** intraday reversal: |Z over the last 60 min| > k, dir = against it (P 2; G 1.5, 2.5); state on the grid; IC = Spearman(-Z_last60, excess Z) over all grid times
* **F1** structure: last 2 confirmed swing highs AND lows strictly rising (HH+HL) or falling (LH+LL); state at every 5-min close (P 2 swings; G 3)
* **F2** break of structure: first 5-min close (P) / wick (G) beyond the last confirmed swing in the F1 trend direction (trend and swing as known at the previous close)
* **F3** change of character: first 5-min close (P) / wick (G) beyond the last confirmed swing AGAINST the prevailing F1 trend; dir = new direction
* **F4** displacement: 5-min range > m x ATR(14 five-min bars before it, prior session included) and close in the outer 20% (P 2; G 1.5, 3)
* **F5** liquidity sweep: previous 5-min close inside, wick beyond prior-day high/low or the last confirmed swing, close back inside (P PDH/PDL + swings; G PDH/PDL only); dir = back into range
* **F6** failed auction: new session high/low, then n consecutive 5-min closes back inside the prior range with no new extreme (P 3 = 15 min; G 2, 6); dir = away from the extreme
* **F7** order block: F4(P) displacement whose close is the first close beyond the last confirmed swing in its direction; block = last opposite-colour 5-min bar before it; event = first 1-min bar trading back into the body (P), wick range (G wick1), first two retests (G body2)
* **F8** fair value gap: bar1.high < bar3.low (bull) / bar1.low > bar3.high (bear), size >= g x sigma_15 at the bar-3 close (P 0.25; G 0.1, 0.5); event = first 1-min bar trading back into the gap; dir = gap direction
* **F9** inverse FVG: first 5-min close (P) / 5-min wick (G) through an F8(P) gap against its direction; dir = closing direction
* **X1** B4(P) alignment effect when GEX(t) < 0 minus when GEX(t) > 0 (G: GEX at 09:45)
* **X2** B2(P) failed-breakout effect when GEX(t) > 0 minus GEX(t) < 0 (G: GEX at 09:45)
* **X5** F5(P) sweep effect when a swept level is within 0.25 sigma_30 of the A4 wall on its side (call wall for high sweeps, put wall for low sweeps) minus elsewhere
* **X6** B3b(P) VWAP-rejection effect when GEX(t) > 0 minus GEX(t) < 0 (G: GEX at 09:45)
* **X12** B1(P) OR-breakout effect when VIX(t) is in the top trailing tercile minus the bottom tercile (cut points from the prior 252 sessions' VIX closes)

## Implementation choices where the registry text left a detail open (fixed before any outcome was computed)

* Event window: only events with 09:45 <= t <= close - 30 min are tested (the Stage-1 grid window; IV_atm and the
  same-bucket unconditional mean exist there). Events before 09:45 (e.g. OR15 breakouts at 09:45 and earlier) and after
  15:30 are dropped, not shifted.
* B2: every fresh break of the OR30 (previous post-OR 5-min close inside), not only the first one; a close beyond the
  OPPOSITE side before any close inside ends the watch without an event.
* B3a: the crossing close is close #1 of the N. B3b: the previous close must be on the approach side of VWAP.
* B4: frame bars aligned at 09:30 (the last 60-min bar of a session is 15:30-16:00); EMAs (span 9/21, recursive,
  seeded with the first close) run continuously over the 10 prior sessions and today; "4 of 5" / "3 of 5" require
  no frame aligned the other way.
* B5: sigma_open-to-t uses IV_atm(t) (the board at t) over the minutes since 09:30; open = the 09:30 1-minute open.
* B6: "5-min Z > 15-min Z per minute" read as |r_5|/5 > |r_15|/15 with r_5, r_15 of the same sign.
* B7: event at close - 30 min (15:30; 12:30 on early closes); outcome h = close. Prior close = last regular-hours
  1-minute close of the prior NYSE session (SPX for SPXW).
* B8 / B5 / B6 / B4 / F1 are states, evaluated at every grid time where they hold (overlapping events; inference
  clusters by day).
* F1-F3: swings within the session only (no prior-day swings); a level can produce one F2/F3 event (its first close /
  wick beyond since confirmation). F2/F3 need a prevailing F1(P) trend.
* F4: ATR = simple mean of the 14 true ranges before the bar; the prior session's 5-min bars seed it (no gap TR).
* F5: approach from inside required (previous 5-min close inside the level); a bar sweeping a high AND a low is
  dropped. X5 "at a wall" = any swept level within 0.25 sigma_30 (log distance) of the call wall (high sweeps) or put
  wall (low sweeps) at t; no wall on that side = "elsewhere"; GEX unavailable = excluded.
* F6: the prior range = the session range before the new extreme; n consecutive closes inside counted from the bar
  after the extreme; a close outside resets the count; a newer extreme replaces the candidate.
* F7: "breaks structure (F2)" = the displacement bar is the first close beyond the last confirmed swing in the
  displacement's own direction (no F1 trend required, so CHoCH-type breaks count). G "first two retests": a second
  retest needs a 1-minute bar fully outside the zone in between.
