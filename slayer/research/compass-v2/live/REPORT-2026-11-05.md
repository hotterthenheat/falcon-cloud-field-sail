# LIVE-SCAN day report, 2026-11-05

**One day proves nothing.** Everything below describes a single session: SPY fell 4.2% open to close (765.8 to 733.4) and VIX rose from 17.3 to 27.5, a crash-type trend day. Contract-minutes are heavily dependent (one index move lifts dozens of strikes at once and the same contract is counted every minute), so effective sample sizes are far smaller than the row counts. Nothing here is validated; the rules at the end are hypotheses to pre-register and test on history and on forward days.

## Reading (hand-written after the close; the tables below are generated by scan_eod.py)

front-expiry book went from negative to positive vendor-sign gamma around noon. One direction dominated all day, so
"what the 50% winners had in common" is mostly "they were puts bought early". Any rule read off this day is first of
all a bet that tomorrow is a crash day too.

**Base rate.** 29.4% of all buyable contract-minutes (216,248 rows, 4,448 contracts, 250 names) later showed a bid
>= 1.5x their entry ask before 16:00 (18.6% if the next snapshot must confirm). Puts 41.8%, calls 16.6%; 0DTE 52.6%;
entries 09:30-10:00 52%, 15:30-16:00 4%. Winners are common on a day like this, so finding them proves nothing.

**Reaching 1.5x is not making money.** Buying every buyable contract-minute with a take-profit at 1.5x and otherwise
holding to 15:59 averaged **-6.9% per trade (-7.6% after $0.65/side)**, even on this day: puts +16%, calls -31%,
0DTE -16% (median +50%: many hit, the rest go to ~0). The edge, if any, is in choosing the side and the time, which
is exactly what is unknown at entry.

**What separated movers once name, side and clock hour are held fixed** (auc_within_name_side_hour; AM and PM

## Candidate rules: to PRE-REGISTER, not validated (do not test on 2026-11-05)

Each goes into the ledger as a spec before any test; DEV = history before 2025, then validation 2025, then the
forward days this recorder accumulates. Success = expectancy per trade after ask-in/bid-out and fees with the
protocol's controls (shuffled-signal and zero-drift nulls, PBO, DSR), NOT the share that reaches 1.5x.

1. **R1 Short-gamma flip crossing.** Mechanism: below Terrain's flip dealers are net short gamma and hedge with the
   move, so a move through the flip accelerates. Trigger: price crosses Terrain's re-priced flip (levelFlip, rebuilt
   from /api/exposure/hist) with the front-expiry book negative; buy the nearest-expiry option on the crossing side,
   |delta| 0.20-0.35, spread <= 8%. Exit: resting limit at +50%, vol-scaled stop, time stop 60 min, exit if price
   re-crosses the flip. Expected sign: positive, larger on days with negative front gamma.
2. **R2 Clear path to the wall.** Mechanism: large gamma strikes pin; an option whose path to the next big-GEX
   strike is long can travel. Trigger: Compass/engine-chosen side, then prefer the contract whose distance to the
   largest |GEX| strike in its direction is >= 1x the 1-hour implied move. Compare against the same side with the wall
   inside 0.5x. Same exit as R1.
3. **R3 Realized outrunning implied.** Mechanism: when realized vol since the open exceeds ATM IV, option sellers are
   underpriced and dealers short those options must chase. Trigger: after 10:30, rv_so_far / ATM IV >= 1.25 and the
   30-min trend in the option's direction; buy |delta| 0.25-0.40 nearest expiry. Exit: trailing stop scaled by realized
   vol, time stop 90 min. Expected sign: positive; null = the same entries with the ratio shuffled across names.
4. **R4 Compass p1 as a side selector.** Mechanism: none new; it tests whether the live engine's score carries the
   side choice. Trigger: engine p1 in its top quintile at a 5-minute mark; buy that contract at the next ask; exit at
   a resting +50% or a vol-scaled stop. Data: the engine's own rows logs (rows/*.jsonl) plus this recorder; forward
   only, since the engine's history starts 2026-10-29.
5. **Trap, not a rule: pullback entries inside the day's trend.** Today's strongest within-hour separation is the
   same VWAP/open pullback that failed in DEV. Do not re-test it without a new mechanism; if it is ever re-registered,
   count it as a re-try of the dead VWAP-pullback family in the trial ledger.
