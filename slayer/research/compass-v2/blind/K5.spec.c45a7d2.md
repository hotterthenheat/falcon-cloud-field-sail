## Rule (verbatim from research/challenger/candidates.py `k5_overnight_spx_hard`)
- Root SPXW only; the overnight session (marks 20:15–09:15 ET belonging to the next regular session).
- Eligible rows: |delta| (col c14) 0.10–0.20; minutes since the overnight open (c1) 225–584, i.e. marks
  00:00–05:59 ET; the side the overnight move favours (c33 = gap vs prior close, signed by the contract's side, > 0).
- Score = 1 − | |delta| − 0.15 |, through the challenger's seat rules (`common.simulate`, line 0): one call a night
  (one root, one seat), the strike nearest 0.15 delta at the first mark where any row is eligible.
- Exit "hard": fill = the ask of the minute quote one minute after the mark; win = the bid reaches 1.50× the fill
  before it falls to 0.4678× (the 0.5322 stop, no break-even move); else the bid at the 09:20 flat. Return =
  exit / fill − 1 (+0.50 on a win, −0.5322 on a stop). Computed from the row columns hit50 / worstPre / flatBid.
- Fee variant: $0.65/contract/side ($1.30 round trip) subtracted.
