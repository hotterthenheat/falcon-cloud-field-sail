# US Retail Options Market Size and Options-Tool Business Models (2023-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-07. Scope: US only. Every figure is marked with its period. Many tool-company figures come from affiliate or aggregator review sites, and these are flagged where used. Private companies in this space (Unusual Whales, SpotGamma, Market Chameleon, OptionStrat, Option Alpha, Tradytics, TradingView) do not publish audited revenue.

## 1. Retail options volume and share (OCC, Cboe, brokers), 2023-2026; 0DTE share of SPX and retail share of 0DTE

### Takeaway
US listed options reached a sixth straight record in 2025: about 15.2B contracts (+24-26% YoY), or about 61M contracts a day. No official retail share exists. The best primary datapoints are these: individual customers make up about 54% of trading on non-expiry days and 73.5% on expiry days (SEC staff), and retail makes up 47-57% of SPX 0DTE volume (Cboe, 2025). Broker volumes are still growing fast into 2026; Robinhood alone traded a record 905M contracts in Q3 2026 (+48% YoY).

### Cited Findings
**Industry totals**
- **OCC 2025 total:** 15,207,163,554 contracts cleared, up 24.4% from 12,224,227,606 in 2024 — [OCC, Jan 5 2026](https://www.theocc.com/newsroom/views/2026/01-05-occ-annual-2025-and-december-2025-volume)
- **OCC annual report:** rounds 2025 to about 15.3B. OCC logged its 32 highest-volume days ever in 2025, two of them above 100M contracts — [OCC 2025 Annual Report](https://annualreport.theocc.com/2025/a-message-from-leadership)
- **Cboe view of 2025 (published Jan 22 2026):**
  - About 15.2B contracts, +26% vs 2024. This is the sixth consecutive record year, at an ADV of about 61M.
  - Single-stock options +28%, ETF options +32%, index options +21% (about 5M/day).
  - SPX averaged a record 3.9M/day and VIX options a record 858K/day. FLEX ADV was 1.4M, up about 62%.
  - Volume topped 70M on 21 days. April 4 2025 was the first day above 100M, and October 10 2025 set the record at 110M.
  - Source: [Cboe, "The State of the Options Industry: 2025"](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/the-state-of-the-options-industry-2025)
  - The 24.4% (OCC) and 26% (Cboe) growth rates differ because the two use different measurement bases.
- **2026 outlook items from Cboe:**
  - More exchanges: MEMX second venue and IEX Options.
  - Extended-hours options and more expirations.
  - Prediction markets and AI as themes.
  - 0DTE ETFs and dispersion/variance futures.
  - Easing of pattern-day-trader rules for small accounts, described as a favorable regulatory trend.
  - Source: [Cboe, Jan 2026](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/the-state-of-the-options-industry-2025)

**0DTE**
- **Full-year 2025:** SPX 0DTE averaged 2.3M contracts/day, or 59% of SPX volume — [Cboe, Jan 2026](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/the-state-of-the-options-industry-2025)
- **May 2025:** 0DTE reached a record share of more than 61% of SPX volume. Retail was 54% of SPX 0DTE volume in May 2025, up from a low of 47% in April 2025 — [Cboe Insights, Jun 2 2025 (Mandy Xu)](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/spx-0-dte-options-jump-to-61-share-on-retail-resurgence). The methodology for identifying retail is not described on the page.
- **Retail share during the April 2025 volatility:** fell from about 57% to 47% in early April 2025 (Cboe, as reported by secondary coverage) — [FOW, May 2025](https://www.fow.com/insights/analysis-cboe-points-to-retail-flow-as-zero-day-options-grow)
- **0DTE as a share of retail options volume:** one outlet reported that 0DTE hit a record 48% of total retail options volume. This is a secondary source and the underlying data provider is unverified — [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/0dte-options-record-retail-volume/)

**Individual and retail share of all options**
- **SEC staff, options market-structure roundtable:**
  - Lists 5,299,576 individual customers who traded options.
  - A secondary summary dates this to the period Nov 2022 through end-2025.
  - Individuals were 73.5% of trading on expiration days vs 54.3% on non-expiry days.
  - Source: [SEC roundtable supporting data](https://www.sec.gov/files/roundtable-options-market-structure.pdf)
- **Aggregator estimates:** retail at about 43-48% of daily options volume. The methodology is unclear and this is not an official figure — [Walnut stats page](https://walnutinvest.com/stats/options-trading-statistics)

**Brokers**
- **Robinhood Q3 2026 (quarter ended Sep 30 2026; released Oct 27 2026):**
  - Options contracts traded: record 905M, +48% YoY.
  - Options revenue: $399M, +31% YoY, out of $867M transaction revenue.
  - Funded customers: 28.7M (+7%).
  - Gold subscribers: record 5.2M (+34%), an 18% adoption rate.
  - Total platform assets: $396B (+19%).
  - ARPU: $200 annualized.

  - Source: [Robinhood Q3 2026 release via MENAFN](https://menafn.com/1111875312/Robinhood-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Results); [Robinhood IR](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-third-quarter-2026-results)
- **Robinhood implied unit economics (my calculation):**
  - Q3 2026: $399M / 905M contracts ≈ $0.44 options revenue per contract.
  - Implied Q3 2025: about 611M contracts and about $305M options revenue.
- **Robinhood October 2025:** options contracts of 266.7M in the month, +69% YoY — [Robinhood Oct 2025 operating data via Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/robinhood-markets-inc-reports-october-2025-operating-data-2025-11-13)
- **Robinhood Q4/FY2025:** search snippets of the Feb 10 2026 release conflict. The release page returned HTTP 403, so these could not be verified — [Robinhood IR](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results)
- **Interactive Brokers 2025 monthly options contracts:**
  - Feb 120.5M (+21.8% YoY)
  - Mar 133.7M (+35.7%)
  - Apr 137.8M (+30.4%)
  - May 128.3M (+23%)

- **Webull:**
  - About 430M options contracts traded on the platform in 2023, per the Feb 2024 merger announcement — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/webull-and-sk-growth-strike-deal-for-nasdaq-listing-in-merger-agreement/)

- **tastytrade, at its 2021 acquisition by IG:** about 105,000 active accounts and 1.3% of all US equity options volume — [IG/tastytrade release, Business Wire, Jan 2021](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210120005810/en)

### Inferences
- Robinhood's 905M contracts in Q3 2026 annualize to about 3.6B contracts. That is roughly 20% or more of the 2025 OCC total, though it is not apples-to-apples because OCC counts both sides and other categories. Robinhood is the single largest retail options venue by contract count.
- The industry is growing about 20-25% a year, and the 0DTE share of SPX is close to 60%. Retail options activity is therefore large, concentrated in short-dated and index products, and still expanding into 2026. That favors tools built around intraday and expiry-day workflows.
- Broker options revenue is about $0.44 per contract at Robinhood. A tool that drives or routes contracts can be monetized through broker referrals; this is pay-per-funded-account economics.

### Gaps
- No official audited retail share of total US options volume exists. Retail-share figures are Cboe estimates or SEC staff counts with limited methodology.
- Schwab options DARTs and share of trades for 2025-2026 were not found in this pass. Schwab reports this in monthly activity reports.
- Full-year 2025 contract totals for Robinhood (unverified, see above), Webull 2025-2026, Moomoo/Futu US, and tastytrade/IG were not found.
- OCC 2026 year-to-date totals were not retrieved. An aggregator headline ("sixth straight record ... 2026 pace") exists but was not verified.

## 2. Number of active retail options traders, account sizes, spending on tools

### Takeaway
There are about 5.3M individual customers who traded US-listed options (SEC staff count). About 21% of US investors say they have traded options (FINRA Foundation, 2024 data). There is no reliable public data on average options-account size or on retail spending on tools. The best proxy for willingness to pay is Robinhood Gold's 5.2M subscribers, an 18% adoption rate as of Q3 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **SEC staff count:** 5,299,576 individual customers traded options — [SEC roundtable data](https://www.sec.gov/files/roundtable-options-market-structure.pdf)
- **FINRA Foundation NFCS investor survey (2024 data, published Nov/Dec 2025):** 43% of investors have an account that allows options trading, but 33% are unsure. 21% have bought or sold options, up from 19% in 2021 — [FINRA Foundation](https://www.finrafoundation.org/sites/finrafoundation/files/2025-11/NFCS_Investor_Survey_Report_White_Paper.pdf)
- **Robinhood Q3 2026:** funded customers 28.7M and ARPU $200/year. Gold subscribers number 5.2M, the paid tier, an 18% adoption rate. Platform assets are $396B, about $13.8K per funded customer (my calculation) — [Robinhood Q3 2026](https://menafn.com/1111875312/Robinhood-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Results)
- **Cboe/Moomoo 2024 Options Trader Survey:** reported increased options popularity. The details were not retrieved — [Seeking Alpha PR](https://seekingalpha.com/pr/19888913-moomoo-cboe-2024-options-trader-survey-shows-increased-options-popularity)

### Inferences
- A serviceable US market of about 5M people trade options. The engaged core that would pay $50-300/month for analytics is likely a small fraction of that, low hundreds of thousands at most. This is an inference; no source counts paying tool subscribers industry-wide.
- Robinhood Gold's 18% paid adoption at about $5/month suggests mass retail will pay small amounts. Premium options analytics at $50-300/month is a much narrower, enthusiast market.

### Gaps
- No primary data on the average options-account size.
- No public industry figure for retail spending on trading tools or data subscriptions.
- No data on how many options traders use third-party analytics.

## 3. Business models and revenues of options-tool companies

### Takeaway
The category runs mostly on retail subscriptions at about $40-300/month, with tiered pricing. Larger players add a second leg: API, data licensing, or institutional seats. Unusual Whales sells an API from $150 to $375/month plus custom enterprise and redistribution licenses. SpotGamma has an institutional desk tier. Broker-routing and affiliate revenue is also used. The only public pure-play, Blackboxstocks, shows the downside: revenue fell from $4.96M (2022) to $3.1M (2023), it carried a going-concern warning, and it attempted a reverse merger into an unrelated EV-parts company. Disclosed revenue for private players is essentially nonexistent.

### Cited Findings
**Unusual Whales**

- **Older or alternate tier list:** Retail Basic $42, Retail Pro $63, Professional $170/month. This comes from a review site and may be outdated — [Bullish Bears review](https://bullishbears.com/unusual-whales-review/)

### Inferences
- **Price bands:**
  - Entry flow and screener tools cluster at $40-60/month (UW, Market Chameleon, OptionStrat, TrendSpider entry).
  - Dealer-positioning and "institutional-style" analytics charge $99-299/month (SpotGamma, Blackbox at $100).
  - API and data tiers run $150-375/month, and enterprise is custom.
- **Strongest monetization pattern:** the clearest example is UW's dual market. Retail subscriptions build the brand and distribution, and the data is resold via API and enterprise licenses to brokers, desks, and AI builders. Licensing is likely the more defensible leg.
- **Blackbox lesson:** a retail-only alert platform has volatile revenue tied to retail activity cycles (2021 peak, then decline) and weak defensibility.

- Affiliate and referral economics, such as broker CPA payouts to tool companies, were not found in primary sources.

## 4. Churn and stickiness (community, data moats, workflow lock-in, broker API integrations)

### Takeaway
Primary churn data for this category is not public. The observable stickiness levers are these:
- Annual-plan discounts: SpotGamma prices annual at about 9 months of monthly.
- Execution integration: UW plus TradeStation lets users trade inside the tool.
- Proprietary or derived data: dealer gamma models, flow classification, redistribution licenses.
- Community and content flywheels.

### Cited Findings
- **Annual-plan discounts:** SpotGamma annual Essential is $891 vs $1,188 billed monthly, and annual Alpha is $2,691 vs $3,588. That is about a 25% discount to push annual commitment — [SpotGamma support](https://support.spotgamma.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500002666102-What-is-the-cost-of-a-SpotGamma-Subscription)
- **Broker integrations:** the UW-TradeStation integration lets users place trades from UW tools — [Business Wire 2023](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230821041895/en/Unusual-Whales-Collaborates-with-TradeStation-Securities-to-Allow-Trading-Through-its-Suite-of-Tools). UW data is also distributed through third-party trading platforms such as the StockSharp connector — [StockSharp](https://stocksharp.com/en/store/unusualwhales/)
- **Activity cyclicality:** retail share of 0DTE dropped from about 57% to 47% in the April 2025 volatility spike, then rebounded to 54% in May — [Cboe](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/spx-0-dte-options-jump-to-61-share-on-retail-resurgence). Usage of retail tools likely follows the same cycle; Blackbox's revenue fell 37% in 2023 — [Blackbox FY2023](https://www.fintechfutures.com/press-releases/blackboxstocks-announces-financial-results-for-the-fourth-quarter-and-year-ended-december-31-2023)

### Inferences
- Retail options tools face activity-driven churn: subscriptions fall when volatility and retail participation drop. Products tied to execution (broker linking, journaling of real fills, automation as in Option Alpha bots) and products with B2B data revenue should churn less than pure alert feeds.
- Broker APIs (Schwab, IBKR, Tradier, tastytrade, Alpaca) let a tool become the trading front end, which raises switching costs. Only UW-TradeStation was evidenced in this pass.

### Gaps
- No published churn, LTV, or retention figures for any options-tool company.
- Terms and availability of broker APIs (Schwab, IBKR, Tradier, tastytrade, Alpaca options) and of broker app marketplaces were not researched in this pass.

## 5. Distribution channels that worked

### Takeaway
Evidence points to social and creator-led distribution, plus affiliate and coupon programs and content syndication. UW grew from an X/Twitter account into a newsletter, podcast, and syndicated content presence. SpotGamma uses affiliates, influencer coupons, and conference (MoneyShow) codes. Quantitative channel-performance data is not public.

### Cited Findings
- **Unusual Whales:** runs a Substack newsletter, a podcast, and Nasdaq.com syndication — [UW Substack](https://unusualwhales.substack.com/p/unusual-whales-api-prices-increasing); [Rephonic podcast listing](https://rephonic.com/podcasts/unusual-whales); [Nasdaq author page](https://www.nasdaq.com/authors/unusual-whales/page/3)
- **SpotGamma:** affiliate program, and checkout coupons tied to MoneyShow 2026 and an influencer (Matt Kohrs) — [SpotGamma affiliate area](https://spotgamma.com/affiliate-area/); [checkout](https://spotgamma.com/register/spotgamma-alpha/?coupon=MATT-KOHRS)
- **Affiliate-driven review sites:** much of the "best options tools" search results are affiliate-driven review and comparison sites, which signals a paid-referral SEO channel — [TradingTools.review](https://tradingtools-directory.pages.dev/best/options-tools/); [Bullish Bears](https://bullishbears.com/unusual-whales-review/)
- **Broker-led media:** tastytrade/tastylive is a broker media network producing daily 0DTE content. One example is a Dec 17 2025 episode on SPX 0DTE dominating retail — [tastylive](https://www.tastylive.com/shows/the-liz-and-jny-show/episodes/the-liz-and-jny-show-spx-zero-dte-trading-dominates-retail-market-12-17-2025)

### Inferences
- The proven playbook is a free, viral social presence (flow screenshots, news on X), then a newsletter and podcast, then a paid tool with an affiliate program. Media-first brokers (tastytrade) show that content can itself be the distribution engine.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on CAC by channel or conversion rates.
- Discord and Reddit effectiveness and broker-marketplace listings were not evidenced with primary sources.
